About CalcAdda

CalcAdda (calcadda.in) is a free online calculator hub built specifically for Indian users. Whether you're planning a home loan, filing your taxes, tracking investments, or converting units — CalcAdda has a tool for it.

Our mission

To make financial and everyday calculations simple, accurate, and accessible to every Indian — student, salaried employee, business owner, or investor. No popups, no paywalls, no mandatory signup required. Everything is free.

Tools available

  • EMI Calculator — home, car and personal loans with affiliate bank links
  • Income Tax Calculator — Old vs New regime FY 2025-26 with Excel export
  • PF / EPF Calculator — EPF corpus, EPS pension, VPF returns
  • GST Calculator — all 4 slabs, CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown
  • SIP Calculator — mutual fund returns with top fund recommendations & links
  • Loan Eligibility Calculator — max loan based on salary and FOIR
  • Unit Converter — length, weight, temperature, area, speed
  • Currency Converter — live exchange rates, 24 currencies
  • CGPA Calculator — weighted GPA and CBSE % conversion
  • Age Calculator — exact age with birthday countdown
  • Percentage Calculator — 4 calculation types
  • Date Calculator — difference, add days, day-of-week finder

Data accuracy

All financial calculators use standard RBI/EPFO/CBDT formulas. Income tax is updated for FY 2025-26 (Budget 2025). EPF uses the EPFO-declared rate of 8.25% for FY 2024-25. Currency rates are fetched live. For major financial decisions, always consult a SEBI-registered advisor or chartered accountant.

Technology

CalcAdda is built with pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no frameworks, no tracking scripts, no personal data collection. Fast, lightweight, works offline after first load. Calculations run entirely in your browser.
