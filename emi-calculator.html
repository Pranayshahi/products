EMI Calculator — FAQs
What is EMI? +
EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed monthly payment you make to repay your loan. Each EMI includes both principal repayment and interest. In the early months, most of the EMI goes toward interest; this ratio shifts toward principal over time.
What is a good home loan interest rate in India? +
As of 2025, home loan rates in India range from 8.50% to 9.5% p.a. SBI and LIC Housing Finance typically offer the lowest rates. A CIBIL score above 750 gets you the best rates. Use the "Apply" buttons above to compare bank offers directly.
Should I choose a longer or shorter loan tenure? +
Longer tenure reduces your monthly EMI but significantly increases total interest paid. For example, a ₹30L loan at 8.5% for 20 years pays ₹33L interest, while for 10 years it pays only ₹15L interest. Shorter tenure saves more money if you can afford the higher monthly payment.
What is an amortization schedule? +
An amortization schedule shows the month-by-month breakdown of your EMI — how much goes toward principal and how much toward interest each month. Click "View amortization schedule" above to see your full schedule.
How does prepayment help? +
Prepaying your loan reduces the outstanding principal directly. Banks typically either reduce your tenure (keeping EMI same) or reduce your EMI (keeping tenure same). Reducing tenure saves significantly more interest overall — even one extra EMI per year makes a big difference.

EMI Calculator — Everything You Need to Know

An EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is a fixed monthly payment you make to repay a loan over a set period. Each payment includes both the principal repayment and the interest component. Our EMI calculator uses the standard RBI-approved formula to compute your exact monthly outflow.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

Where: P = Principal loan amount, r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = Total number of monthly instalments (years × 12)

Tips to Lower Your EMI

  • Maintain a CIBIL score above 750 — banks offer their lowest rates to high-score applicants
  • Choose a longer tenure (20–30 years) to reduce monthly EMI, though total interest paid increases
  • Make a larger down payment (20–30%) to reduce the principal and hence EMI
  • Consider part-prepayment when you receive a bonus — most home loans allow this without penalties
  • Compare rates across multiple banks — even 0.25% difference on ₹50L saves over ₹1L over 20 years
