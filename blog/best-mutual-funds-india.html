How to Choose the Right Fund

  1. Define your goal — retirement (20+ years), child education (10 years), home purchase (5 years), emergency fund (1-3 years)
  2. Assess your risk tolerance — can you stay invested through a -40% drop? If yes, small cap; if no, balanced advantage
  3. Always choose Direct Plan — 0.5–1.0% lower expense ratio compounding over 20 years adds 10–20% more corpus
  4. Use XIRR, not CAGR — for SIPs, XIRR accurately measures your personal return accounting for investment dates
  5. Don't over-diversify — 3–4 funds across categories is enough. 15 funds = confusion, not diversification
Tax on mutual fund gains (2025):
Equity funds held > 1 year: 12.5% LTCG (Long Term Capital Gains) above ₹1.25 lakh per year — after Budget 2024 change.
Debt funds: Taxed at income tax slab rate regardless of holding period.
