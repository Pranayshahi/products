How to Calculate EMI in India — Complete Guide 2025

📅 2026-01-15 ⏱️ 8 min read 📍 CalcAdda.in
EMIHome LoanPersonal FinanceFinance Guide

If you've ever applied for a loan in India, you've heard the term EMI — but how is it actually calculated? This guide breaks down the EMI formula step by step, with real examples for home loans, car loans and personal loans.

Quick answer: EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months.

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed monthly payment you make to repay a loan. Unlike a simple interest payment, each EMI includes both:

  • Principal repayment — reducing the outstanding loan balance
  • Interest component — interest charged on the outstanding balance

The ratio between principal and interest changes every month. In the early months, most of your EMI goes toward interest. As the loan matures, more goes toward principal — this is called amortization.

The EMI Formula Explained

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

Where:

  • P = Principal loan amount (in ₹)
  • r = Monthly interest rate = Annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100
  • n = Total number of monthly instalments = Years × 12

Worked Example: ₹30 Lakh Home Loan

Loan: ₹30,00,000 at 8.5% p.a. for 20 years

  • P = ₹30,00,000
  • r = 8.5 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.007083
  • n = 20 × 12 = 240 months

EMI = 30,00,000 × 0.007083 × (1.007083)²⁴⁰ ÷ ((1.007083)²⁴⁰ − 1)

= 30,00,000 × 0.007083 × 5.281 ÷ (5.281 − 1)

= 30,00,000 × 0.007083 × 1.233

= ₹26,035 per month

Total paid: ₹26,035 × 240 = ₹62,48,400
Total interest paid: ₹62,48,400 − ₹30,00,000 = ₹32,48,400 (108% of principal!)

What Happens in Each EMI: First vs Last Month

For the ₹30L example above, here's the split of your first and last EMI:

MonthEMIInterestPrincipalBalance
Month 1₹26,035₹21,250₹4,785₹29,95,215
Month 120 (10 yr)₹26,035₹14,210₹11,825₹19,86,000
Month 240 (20 yr)₹26,035₹183₹25,852₹0

Notice how in Month 1, ₹21,250 of your ₹26,035 EMI goes to interest. By Month 240, only ₹183 is interest. This is why making prepayments early in the loan saves the most money.

How to Reduce Your EMI

  1. Negotiate a lower rate — A 0.5% reduction on ₹50L saves ₹3,700/month and ₹8.9L over 20 years
  2. Increase your down payment — 30% down vs 20% down reduces EMI by ~12.5%
  3. Improve CIBIL score — Scores above 750 qualify for the lowest rates
  4. Extend tenure — 20 years vs 15 years reduces EMI by ~18% (but increases total interest)
  5. Transfer to a lower rate — Home loan balance transfer to a bank offering 0.5% lower is worth it if 5+ years remain
Prepayment tip: Prepaying ₹1 lakh in Year 1 of a ₹30L/20-year loan saves over ₹2.8 lakh in interest and reduces tenure by ~14 months. The same prepayment in Year 15 saves much less.
