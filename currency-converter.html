Currency Converter — FAQs
What is today's USD to INR rate? +
The USD to INR rate is updated live on this page using open exchange rate APIs. As of early 2026, 1 USD ≈ ₹83–85. Select USD → INR above to see the current live rate.
Are these exchange rates live? +
Yes, rates are fetched from open.er-api.com which updates rates daily. These are mid-market rates. Actual rates from banks or money exchangers may differ slightly due to their spread/margin.
What is 1 AED in Indian Rupees? +
1 AED ≈ ₹22.7 (approximate, as of early 2026). This is important for the large Indian expat community in the UAE. Select AED → INR above for the current live rate.
