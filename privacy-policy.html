Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Information we collect

CalcAdda.in does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All calculations happen entirely in your browser (client-side JavaScript). No data is sent to any server.

Cookies

We do not use any cookies for tracking, analytics, or personalisation. If Google AdSense is enabled in the future, Google may place its own cookies as per their privacy policy.

Currency data

The currency converter fetches exchange rates from open.er-api.com via a standard HTTP request. Your IP address may be visible to this service as part of a normal web request. No personal data beyond a standard IP address is transmitted.

Third-party links

CalcAdda.in contains affiliate links to bank websites (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, etc.) and mutual fund websites (Quant, Nippon, PPFAS, etc.). When you click these links and visit third-party websites, their own privacy policies apply. CalcAdda.in is not responsible for their data practices.

Children's privacy

Our calculators are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
