CalcAdda (calcadda.in) is a free Indian calculator hub with 14 tools — EMI, income tax, PF/EPF, GST, SIP, CGPA, currency, unit converter and more. No signup, no paywalls.
Are CalcAdda calculators accurate? +
Yes. All financial calculators use standard formulas and official CBDT/EPFO/RBI guidelines. Tax calculations are updated for FY 2025-26. Currency rates are fetched live. Always consult a certified financial advisor for major decisions.
Is CalcAdda completely free? +
Yes — all 14 calculators on CalcAdda are completely free, with no hidden charges, subscriptions, or mandatory signups.
Which calculators are most searched in India? +
EMI calculator, income tax calculator and GST calculator are the most searched financial tools in India. SIP and EPF calculators are popular among investors and salaried employees respectively.
